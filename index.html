Recreation systems

Trail report: Matt

Mountain-bike edition of the flight manual. Conditions: leafy, festive, and slightly ambitious.

  • Green — the driveway

    Warm-up lap. Helmet optional only in the sense that the mailbox is very close.

  • Blue — “it’s just a little rooty”

    Famous last words, spoken with confidence and a full bottle of water.

  • Black — Plaid mode on the downhill

    Hands stay loose. Eyes stay up. Gravity is doing most of the engineering.

  • Double black — “I still have a good dentist”

    A respectable personal limit, and honestly a strong mission-success criterion.

Hold-my-beer is not a range-approved go/no-go poll. Suspension travel is both emotional and literal. Range anxiety, if any, is in the legs.
